If you are thinking about suicide, hurting yourself, or are in danger, please reach out for immediate help. You deserve support from a real person right now.

United States: call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7, or chat at 988lifeline.org. Text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line. Call 911 for emergencies. Domestic violence hotline: 1-800-799-7233. Trevor Project for LGBTQ+ young people: 1-866-488-7386.

Outside the United States: findahelpline.com lists free, confidential crisis lines in many countries.

On campus: most universities have a counseling center with free or low-cost sessions, and many have after-hours crisis lines. Resident advisors, the dean of students office, and campus health services can also help connect you with support.

When to consider professional help. Consider talking with a counselor, therapist or doctor if difficult feelings last more than two weeks, interfere with school, work or relationships, involve using alcohol or drugs to cope, or if you're having thoughts of self-harm. Asking for help is a sign of strength.

This AI companion is not a substitute for professional care, diagnosis or treatment.
